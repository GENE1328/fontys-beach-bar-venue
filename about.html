The Fonty's feeling

A beachside welcome, made personal.

A place with a point of view

Come for the coast. Stay for the company.

Fonty's Beach Bar & Venue is founded and owned by April Sardi, with a simple idea at its heart: create a welcoming place where people can relax, connect and enjoy the coast together.

In Gaire Village, the beach is part of the experience. Outdoor seating, open air and friendly hospitality make the venue feel easy from the moment you arrive.
